# Foodland Ontario

## Logo Use for Local Farmers
Ontario farmers, processors, and food businesses can use the Foodland Ontario logo completely free of charge. [1, 2] 

* 
* Application Process: Producers must submit a Foodland Ontario Logo Application online. [2, 3] 
* Qualification: The food products must strictly meet the established Ontario food definitions (grown, raised, or produced in Ontario). [2, 4] 
* Usage Permissions: Once approved under a logo use agreement, farmers can feature the logo on product packaging, company websites, and social media. [1, 3] 
* Free Resources: Approved partners gain access to complimentary point-of-sale materials (like price cards and stickers) to help their items stand out in retail environments. [3] 
* Inquiries: Questions regarding licensing can be emailed directly to foodland.omafra@ontario.ca. [1] 
* 

## Seasonal Produce Availability
Consumers and farmers can track when specific fruits and vegetables are fresh using the official Foodland Ontario Availability Guide. [5, 6] 

* 
* Spring: Asparagus, radishes, rhubarb, and greenhouse crops like cucumbers and tomatoes.
* Summer: Berries (strawberries, raspberries, blueberries), cherries, sweet corn, zucchini, beans, and peaches.
* Fall: Apples, pears, pumpkins, squash, grapes, and root vegetables.
* Year-Round: Mushrooms, root vegetables (carrots, onions, rutabagas), and greenhouse produce remain widely available through the winter. [6, 7, 8, 9, 10] 
* 

## Where to Find Corresponding Recipes
Foodland Ontario curates thousands of dishes specifically designed around provincial harvest schedules. [11, 12, 13] 

* 
* Online Database: The Foodland Ontario Recipes Portal lets you filter meals by specific local ingredients, meal types, and cooking times.
* Seasonal Collections: Curated lists of fresh ideas are updated dynamically on the Foodland Ontario Seasonal Recipes Page.
* Annual Calendar: Foodland Ontario distributes a popular physical recipe calendar to local grocery stores each fall. The digital versions and monthly breakdowns can be accessed on the Foodland Ontario Calendar Recipes Page. [9, 11, 12, 14] 
*
